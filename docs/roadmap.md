# What's next

Things we're working on for the next version of the robot.

## Arm
A robotic arm, so PersonaAI can point, wave and gesture while it talks.

## Hearing where you are
A microphone array. The robot hears where a voice comes from, turns toward that person and focuses on their voice, even in a noisy room.

## Camera in the eye
The camera moves into the eye, so the robot really looks at you when it follows your face.

## AI on the robot
Running the AI on the robot itself instead of in the cloud. Faster answers, and it keeps working without internet.

## Louder, clearer voice
Better speakers and an amplifier, so it can be heard at busy events.

## Simpler power
One power supply for the whole robot instead of several.
